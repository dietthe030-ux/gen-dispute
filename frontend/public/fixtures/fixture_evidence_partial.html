Buyer Delivery Inspection Report

Unboxing Report

I received a vintage Rolex Submariner and its automatic movement is running. However, there are significant discrepancies from the listing. It did not come with the original box or papers and was shipped in generic packaging. A watchmaker also verified that the dial has been repainted, rather than being the original dial claimed in the listing. The watch identity matches, but these missing and altered details materially reduce its collector value.

Observed Attributes:

  • Identity: Vintage Rolex Submariner matching the listed model.
  • Movement: Automatic movement is running.
  • Dial: Repainted/Refinished dial (not the original factory dial).
  • Box & Papers: Missing. None included (generic packaging only).
  • Condition: Serviced, but fails key listing claims (original dial, box/papers).
